About Us

Country Line Farming is a farming roleplay community built around teamwork, fair play, and good company. Whether you are a seasoned farmer or just planting your first field, there is a place for you here. We run events, support tickets, and a staff team that keeps things friendly and fair.

Server Rules

1. Be respectful. Treat every member and staff member with kindness.
2. No harassment or hate. Racism, slurs, bullying, and threats are not tolerated.
3. Play fair. No cheating, exploiting bugs, or abusing mods.
4. Follow staff. Staff instructions are final while you are in the server.
5. Stay on topic. Keep chats and voice channels to their purpose.
6. No advertising. Do not promote other servers or services without permission.
7. No spam. Avoid flooding chat with repeated messages or mass pings.
8. Use tickets. Do not ping the Ownership Team. Open a ticket in the support channel instead.
